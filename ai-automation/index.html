What does it cost?

The admin check call is free. If there's something worth building, you get a clear written scope and price before any work starts, and you decide. No surprises.

What actually happens on the free call?

You pick a time that suits you and Nehem phones you. It takes about 20 minutes: you talk through where the time goes (calls, quotes, bookings, invoices, paperwork) and you come away knowing which two or three jobs are worth automating first, whether or not you go ahead. No sales script.

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You explain how your business runs in plain English, and the tech side is handled for you. You'll never be asked to set anything up yourself.

Will it sound like a robot to my customers?

No. Messages are written in your voice and you approve them before anything goes live. Anything unusual or sensitive is handed to a real person: you.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. The work starts with the tools you already use, and something new is only suggested if it's clearly worth it.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's not the aim. The aim is to take repetitive admin off your team so they can spend more time on customers and the work that matters. You decide what gets automated.

What if something breaks?

Every automation is built with checks and alerts, so problems get noticed quickly, and you have a direct number for the person who built it.

Is my customers' data safe?

Only the access a job needs is requested, nothing goes live without your OK, and personal information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. See the Privacy Policy.
