What does it cost?

The discovery call is free. Every business starts from a different place, so after the call you get a clear written scope and price before any work starts, and you decide. No surprises.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I need a website?

Not always, and you'll get a straight answer on the call. A Facebook page is a good start, but it doesn't show up well in Google searches, it can't take proper bookings, and you don't control it. For most local businesses a simple website plus a well set-up Google Business Profile is what turns searches into enquiries.

I already have a website. Can you work with it?

Yes. Often the site itself is fine and what's missing is the rest: a Google profile, online booking, enquiry follow-up or the basics of SEO. If the site is worth keeping, the work starts there instead of from scratch.

How long does it take?

It depends on where you're starting and what you need. You'll get the timing in your written plan before any work starts, so you know what to expect.

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You know your business and your customers; the tech side is handled for you, in plain English. You'll never be asked to set anything up yourself.

Do you run ads as well?

Setting up ads is part of what's on offer, once the basics are ready to turn clicks into enquiries. Paying for ads that land on a page that can't take a booking usually wastes money, so the order matters.

Can I add AI automation later?

Yes. Once the website, Google profile and booking are working, the same person can add AI automation: missed calls answered, quotes followed up, reminders and invoices handled.

Is my customers' data safe?

Only the access a job needs is requested, nothing goes live without your OK, and personal information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. See the Privacy Policy.
