AI automation for cleaning businesses

Cleaning is a fast-reply business. The first company to send a sensible quote usually gets the job, and the regular clients are where the real money is. Both depend on follow-up that's easy to automate.

Sound familiar?

  • Quote requests come in while you're on a clean and get answered at night.
  • A cleaner calls in sick and the morning becomes a phone marathon.
  • Regular clients pause 'for a few weeks' and never come back.

What you could be missing out on

5 things most cleaning businesses could hand to automation

Every business is different, so treat this as a starting list. On the free call we work out which of these would actually make a difference for you, and which wouldn't.

  1. 01

    Fast replies to quote requests

    What's probably happening
    People ask three cleaners for a quote and usually go with the first good reply.
    What we'd set up
    Quote requests get an instant reply asking the right questions (property size, bedrooms, bathrooms, frequency, end-of-lease or regular) and a ballpark from your own price guide for you to confirm.
    What it saves you
    More quotes accepted, without stopping a clean to type.
  2. 02

    Booking confirmations and access details

    What's probably happening
    Keys, codes, pets and parking get sorted by text, one client at a time.
    What we'd set up
    Clients get a confirmation and a reminder before each clean, with a quick form for access details that lands on the job.
    What it saves you
    Fewer wasted trips and fewer messages back and forth.
  3. 03

    Roster changes and cover

    What's probably happening
    When someone calls in sick, finding cover and telling clients eats the morning.
    What we'd set up
    Available staff are messaged in order until someone accepts, and affected clients are told automatically if times change.
    What it saves you
    Sick days that don't take over your morning.
  4. 04

    Regular clients who lapse

    What's probably happening
    Regular clients who pause rarely restart unless someone checks in.
    What we'd set up
    Paused clients get a friendly check-in after a few weeks, and one-off clients are offered a regular booking.
    What it saves you
    More recurring work from people who already know you.
  5. 05

    Invoices and overdue payments

    What's probably happening
    Small invoices are easy to forget and awkward to chase.
    What we'd set up
    Invoices go out after each clean or each month, with polite reminders on overdue ones.
    What it saves you
    Money in on time without the chasing.
