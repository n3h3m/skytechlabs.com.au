What you could be missing out on

5 things most physio and allied health clinics could hand to automation

Every business is different, so treat this as a starting list. On the free call we work out which of these would actually make a difference for you, and which wouldn't.

  1. 01

    Patients who don't rebook

    What's probably happening
    Patients leave without booking their next visit and fall through the cracks.
    What we'd set up
    Anyone without a next appointment gets a reminder to rebook, timed to their plan, with an easy booking link.
    What it saves you
    More patients finishing their care, and a steadier diary.
  2. 02

    No-shows and late cancellations

    What's probably happening
    Missed appointments are lost time you can't get back.
    What we'd set up
    Confirmations and reminders go out before every appointment, and cancelled slots are offered to a waitlist.
    What it saves you
    Fewer empty slots and less phone time for the front desk.
  3. 03

    Referral letters and reports

    What's probably happening
    Letters back to GPs and reports for insurers get written at the end of a long day.
    What we'd set up
    Your notes are turned into a draft letter in your template for you to check and send. You always review before anything goes out.
    What it saves you
    Less after-hours typing, and referrers who hear back promptly.
  4. 04

    New-patient enquiries

    What's probably happening
    "Do I need a referral?", "Do you bulk bill?", "Is there parking?" asked again and again.
    What we'd set up
    Common questions are answered instantly in your clinic's words, and new patients get intake forms before their first visit.
    What it saves you
    A calmer front desk and first appointments that start on time.
  5. 05

    Feedback and reviews

    What's probably happening
    Patients who had a great outcome are rarely asked to say so.
    What we'd set up
    Patients get a short feedback request after discharge, with review requests handled in line with Ahpra guidance.
    What it saves you
    Useful feedback, and more recent reviews, done properly.

Works with what you've got. Already using Cliniko, Nookal, Splose, Halaxy, Coreplus or Power Diary? We start with your existing tools and check what they can connect to before suggesting anything new.

You stay in charge. Clinical decisions and anything you send to referrers stay in your hands. Patient information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
