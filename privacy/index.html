Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

SkyTech Labs ("we", "us", "our") is based in Sydney, Australia. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you deal with us, why we collect it, and how you can access or correct it or make a complaint.

1. What we collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for our business. Depending on how you deal with us, this may include:

  • Enquiry details you enter in our enquiry form: your name, business email address, phone number, business name, what you would like to automate, any additional information you choose to provide, and whether you would like a strategy call or an email reply.
  • Communications you send us by email or phone, and notes from calls and meetings with you.
  • Technical information that is processed automatically when you use the website, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is handled by our hosting and security providers to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.

Please don't include sensitive information (such as health information) or other people's personal information in the form unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

2. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you submit our enquiry form, email or call us, or work with us. When you submit the form, we also record the date and time, the page you submitted it from and the country your request came from, and we use a one-way hashed (non-reversible) version of your IP address for a short time to limit spam and abuse.

Cookies and analytics: this website does not set cookies, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and does not load third-party scripts or fonts.

3. Why we collect, use and disclose it

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and arrange the strategy call you asked for;
  • prepare proposals and provide our services if you engage us;
  • keep records of our dealings with you; and
  • protect our website and systems from spam, fraud and misuse.

We will not add you to a marketing list or send you marketing emails unless you ask us to. If we ever send you marketing, each message will include an easy way to opt out. We do not sell or rent personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our website and business. They process personal information on our behalf, only as needed to provide their services:

  • Cloudflare: receives and stores enquiry form submissions and protects the form from abuse;
  • GitHub: hosts this website and handles the technical information described above; and
  • our email and productivity providers: deliver and store our email and business records.

We may also disclose personal information if required or authorised by law.

5. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. When we use overseas providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle personal information consistently with the APPs.

6. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access limited to the people who need it, and reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete or de-identify it.

7. Anonymity

You can browse this website without telling us who you are, and you can ask general questions without identifying yourself. To respond to an enquiry or arrange a call, however, we need a way to contact you.

8. Access and correction

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@skytechlabs.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable time. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why, unless it would be unreasonable to do so.

9. Complaints

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first at hello@skytechlabs.com.au and we will try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response within 30 days, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
